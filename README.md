# 🌾 HarvestID

> **Every Harvest Has an Identity.**

HarvestID is an AI-powered crop traceability platform that enables farmers to create a **Digital Crop Passport** for every harvest. By documenting farming activities through text, photos, and voice notes, HarvestID transforms simple farm records into a transparent and verifiable crop journey.

Instead of relying on trust alone, buyers can scan a QR code to instantly access a read-only Digital Crop Passport containing the crop's origin, cultivation history, AI-generated insights, and traceability score.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Farmers often struggle to prove the authenticity and quality of their produce, while buyers have limited visibility into a crop's origin and cultivation process. Traditional paper records are difficult to maintain, verify, and share, leading to reduced transparency and trust across the agricultural supply chain.

HarvestID solves this problem by providing an AI-powered digital documentation system that creates a complete and verifiable identity for every crop.

## 👨‍🌾 Farmer Workflow

1. Register a new crop.
2. Enter crop details and farm information.
3. Record farming activities using text, photos, or voice notes.
4. Let AI organize and enhance farming records.
5. Generate a Digital Crop Passport.
6. Share the passport using a QR code.

---

## 🛒 Buyer Workflow

1. Scan the QR code.
2. View the Digital Crop Passport.
3. Explore the farming timeline.
4. Verify crop origin and cultivation history.
5. Review AI-generated insights and traceability score.
